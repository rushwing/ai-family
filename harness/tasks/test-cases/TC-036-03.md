2. Supply discovery errors before and at max_attempts; preserve original capture inputs and retry against a newer snapshot using a fresh epoch. No implicit retry, sleep, network or clock may occur.
3. Inject fixed store validation errors and unexpected operational errors; verify classification, redaction and stale confirmation. Test result epoch/count for invalid epoch, invalid capture, overflow, exhausted discovery and successful filtering.

## Expected results

Success epoch is the returned pinned epoch and count is the number of eligible unique published events. Failure epoch is the requested epoch after exact-positive-integer validation (otherwise None), never evidence of publication and never usable to switch the live path. Failure count is min(input list length, configured capacity) after admission/list validation, otherwise zero; it includes duplicates/filtered events and is not an exact overflow size. Failed request epochs are not pinned. Store epoch_regression becomes terminal epoch_conflict. Deterministic store validation errors (invalid_state, incomplete_snapshot, invalid_event, invalid_integer, invalid_identifier, invalid_context, identity_mismatch, unexpected_intent, forbidden, clock_regression) become terminal invalid_input. Unexpected store errors remain retryable store_failure and exhaust at the configured attempt bound. Only confirmed invalidation reports state_stale=true.
