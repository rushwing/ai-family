## Steps

1. Publish two successive snapshots, updating desired intent between the detached read and publication. Compare reports, retained current intent, per-device revisions and timestamps.
2. Race complete-home reads against fifty publications. Reject equal/older epochs and candidates at the stored intent ordering floor; then publish a higher epoch.
3. Submit an incomplete baseline or a late invalid buffered update. Inspect every device after rejection. Set stale repeatedly, perform ordinary live writes, and recover by successful reconciliation.

## Expected results

Readers see complete publications, each registered device advances exactly one revision per publication, and intent is read under the publication lock. Rejected store publications do not mutate states/revisions/timestamps/epoch/freshness. Explicit stale overrides TTL/uninitialized and persists until reconciliation succeeds. The composition owner's barrier, capture closure and epoch persistence remain required preconditions; these tests do not certify an external transport owner.
