## Initial observed discrepancy (historical)

The three historical REQ-023 provenance records contain SHA256 digests for
`anyio-4.15.1-py3-none-any.whl`, `certifi-2026.7.22-py3-none-any.whl` and
`h11-0.16.0-py3-none-any.whl` that do not match the official PyPI wheel or sdist
digests for those releases. Original dependency archives have not been recovered.
The cause and the identities of the actual dependency bytes remain unknown;
do not infer that this is a benign platform difference or malicious modification.

## Recovery and resolution — 2026-10-15

[human-001] “Fix all findings” delegates this follow-up to generator-001. Contrary
to the earlier unrecovered-bytes assumption, this host retains nine REQ-023
wheelhouses and their download logs. [generator-001] Rehashed the retained
archives: all three match the historically recorded SHA256 exactly. Read-only
fresh downloads from the piwheels URLs in those logs match those same hashes;
the new official PyPI downloads have the different official hashes already
recorded in REQ-025. All nine retained download logs identify the piwheels files.

[Recovery audit](../evidence/REQ-023-recovered-wheel-audit.json) records local
archive identities, current piwheels URL/hash, official PyPI URL/hash, sanitized
log URL excerpts/log digests and ZIP member comparisons. anyio and certifi have
identical complete member sets and byte-identical contents; differing ZIP member
timestamps explain their archive-byte mismatch. h11 differs only in WHEEL's
setuptools generator version (80.9.0 versus official 78.1.0), its corresponding
RECORD and ZIP timestamps; all Python payloads are identical.

The observed cause is mixed-index acquisition: the former builder inherited
both PyPI and piwheels and selected differently packaged piwheels archives.
This is an observed archive/source distinction, not an inference from hashes
alone. The historical digests correctly identify those retained piwheels bytes;
they must not be presented as official PyPI wheel digests. No historical record
is rewritten. Local retained logs are corroborating evidence, not a signed
chain of custody or a new certification of historical runtime execution.

REQ-025 now forces official-index acquisition without pip config/PIP_* option
overrides/cache and verifies exact official hashes before installation. The
fresh eight-wheel closure passes; hermetic mismatch/error tests fail closed.
Mark this identity/provenance discrepancy resolved under the explicit repair
authority, owner generator-001, with TC-025-02 prevention coverage. This does
not supply external approval of the still-proposed REQ-025 increment or alter
archived REQ-023/024 acceptance. Earlier open/unrecovered statements above are
historical and superseded by this attributed recovery.
