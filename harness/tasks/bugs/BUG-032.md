## Observed discrepancy

The three historical REQ-023 provenance records contain SHA256 digests for
`anyio-4.15.1-py3-none-any.whl`, `certifi-2026.7.22-py3-none-any.whl` and
`h11-0.16.0-py3-none-any.whl` that do not match the official PyPI wheel or sdist
digests for those releases. Original dependency archives have not been recovered.
The cause and the identities of the actual dependency bytes remain unknown;
do not infer that this is a benign platform difference or malicious modification.
