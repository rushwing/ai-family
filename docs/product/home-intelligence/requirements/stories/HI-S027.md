# HI-S027 — Verify provider contracts with mock devices

## Context

This R1 backend slice contributes to [HI-F009](../features/HI-F009.md). Existing legacy story references express related scope; they do not prove runtime delivery. See [CROSSWALK](../../CROSSWALK.md).

## Outcome

Verify provider contracts with mock devices. After merge this contract, service or verification increment is available independently; later callers may integrate it without enabling unfinished device writes.

## Scope

- Reusable contract checks cover discovery, stable identity, availability, schema validation and action results.
- Idempotency, timeout, offline and invalid-action cases have explicit assertions.
- The suite runs in CI without HA or physical hardware and reports required checks as executed rather than skipped.

## Non-goals

Other stories in the parent feature; R2 workflow behavior; new UI screens; arbitrary vendor actions; weakened authorization or confirmation.
