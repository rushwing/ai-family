# HI-F107 — Laundry Workflow

## Outcome

Laundry completion notifies a member and governs rack lowering and raising through explicit waits and confirmation.

## User / System Value

Laundry completion notifies a member and governs rack lowering and raising through explicit waits and confirmation. This capability is independently demonstrable through the scenario below.

## Scope

- The reviewed flow defines completion trigger, notification, bounded wait, lowering and confirmation before raising.
- Unknown occupancy/obstruction or unavailable rack state blocks unsafe movement.
- Restart preserves wait and confirmation state.

## Non-goals

Detecting laundry completion through a new camera model.

## Architecture Boundaries

Inherit [architecture alignment](../../ARCHITECTURE_ALIGNMENT.md), ADR-016 and ADR-010. Provider identifiers stay in adapters and ProviderBinding; device writes remain governed asynchronous tasks.
