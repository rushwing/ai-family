# HI-F104 — Human Confirmation

## Outcome

A workflow pauses for an authorized human decision and resumes or terminates safely.

## User / System Value

A workflow pauses for an authorized human decision and resumes or terminates safely. This capability is independently demonstrable through the scenario below.

## Scope

- Confirmation is bound to user/session, run, step and exact action parameters.
- Agent-generated, expired, changed-payload and replayed confirmations are rejected.
- Approval waits survive restart and have bounded expiry.

## Non-goals

Replacing gateway confirmation with a workflow-only flag.

## Architecture Boundaries

Inherit [architecture alignment](../../ARCHITECTURE_ALIGNMENT.md), ADR-016 and ADR-010. Provider identifiers stay in adapters and ProviderBinding; device writes remain governed asynchronous tasks.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Confirmation is bound to user/session, run, step and exact action parameters.
- [ ] Agent-generated, expired, changed-payload and replayed confirmations are rejected.
- [ ] Approval waits survive restart and have bounded expiry.
