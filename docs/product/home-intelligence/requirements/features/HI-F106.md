# HI-F106 — Aquarium Workflow

## Outcome

Lighting and feeding respect time, recent feeding history and safety limits.

## User / System Value

Lighting and feeding respect time, recent feeding history and safety limits. This capability is independently demonstrable through the scenario below.

## Scope

- A typed Feedable contract and provider must be defined during R2 refinement.
- Durable feeding history, idempotency and configured limits prevent duplicate feeding on retry/restart.
- Unknown feeding outcomes require reconciliation or human review before another feed.

## Non-goals

Assuming a retry is safe after an uncertain physical feeding result.

## Architecture Boundaries

Inherit [architecture alignment](../../ARCHITECTURE_ALIGNMENT.md), ADR-016 and ADR-010. Provider identifiers stay in adapters and ProviderBinding; device writes remain governed asynchronous tasks.
