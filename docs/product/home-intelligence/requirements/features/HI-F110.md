# HI-F110 — Household Notification

## Outcome

Workflow notifications reach authorized members through the existing notification boundary.

## User / System Value

Workflow notifications reach authorized members through the existing notification boundary. This capability is independently demonstrable through the scenario below.

## Scope

- Reuse notify.out and approved channel adapters with scoped recipients.
- Outbox/idempotency prevents duplicate delivery where channel semantics allow; uncertain delivery is explicit.
- Retries and dead-letter outcomes are observable without logging credentials.

## Non-goals

Direct Telegram calls from workflow business logic; building every R10 interaction channel.

## Architecture Boundaries

Inherit [architecture alignment](../../ARCHITECTURE_ALIGNMENT.md), ADR-016 and ADR-010. Provider identifiers stay in adapters and ProviderBinding; device writes remain governed asynchronous tasks.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Reuse notify.out and approved channel adapters with scoped recipients.
- [ ] Outbox/idempotency prevents duplicate delivery where channel semantics allow; uncertain delivery is explicit.
- [ ] Retries and dead-letter outcomes are observable without logging credentials.
